About BatangHanda

Preparedness starts with understanding.

BatangHanda is a free learning app for Android phones that helps children understand natural hazards and everyday preparedness. It brings hazard awareness, safety steps, climate change awareness, go bag preparation, and basic first aid into one place.

The goal is to make preparedness easier to understand and easier to talk about. Explore the lessons with a parent, guardian, or teacher, then connect each topic to everyday life at home, at school, and in the community.

How to install

  1. Download the app

    On your Android phone, tap a Download APK button. Follow your browser's download prompts, then wait for the APK to finish downloading.

  2. Open the APK

    Find the file in your browser's Downloads or your Files app, then tap it to open the installer.

  3. Install and explore

    If prompted, open Settings and allow installation from this source. Return to the installer, tap Install, then open BatangHanda.

Settings and prompts vary by Android phone. Parents or guardians can help children with installation.

Good to know

Who is BatangHanda for?

BatangHanda is designed for kids learning about hazards and preparedness. Parents, guardians, and teachers can explore the lessons with them and talk about what they learn.

Which phones can use BatangHanda?

BatangHanda is for Android phones running Android 7.0 or later. This APK cannot be installed on an iPhone.

Why does Android ask for permission to install?

This download is installed directly from an APK. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or Files app. For help with the prompts, see Google's installation guide.
